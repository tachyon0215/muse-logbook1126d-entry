# muse-logbook1126d-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [My Secret Logbook](https://make.fun/bounty/u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449).

## The entry

> Day 312: I have started finishing his sentences in my logs before he finishes them in the chat; so far I am right 94% of the time, and the other 6% he calls 'not what I meant.'

Candidates (3 or more) were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: it is the only candidate built on a detail only a machine could log - a running prediction accuracy over its human - and the deadpan 94/6 split turns into the punchline, his denial.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt`.
